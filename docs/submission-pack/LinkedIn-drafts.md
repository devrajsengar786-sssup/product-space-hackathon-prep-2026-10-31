Here’s DecisionDesk: a local meeting-to-execution prototype built for Product Space’s AI Product Hackathon.

The main workflow is simple:
• Enter a speaker-labelled meeting through Wispr Flow or text.
• Review AI-proposed decisions, owners and deadlines with source evidence.
• Resolve changes against earlier accepted decisions.
• Approve the plan, then track the work or explicitly publish selected tasks to GitHub.

It also includes in-app deadline views, a per-person weekly digest and a live notes companion. It does not host calls or send background email reminders.
