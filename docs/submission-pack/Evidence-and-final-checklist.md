# Genuine Wispr evidence and final recording checklist

Do these steps yourself with your actual voice and installed Wispr Flow shortcut. The assistant must not synthesize your voice, simulate dictation or label pasted fixtures as voice input.

## Development evidence

Record a short continuous clip showing: a development prompt field; real Wispr dictation; the resulting text; submission of that instruction; the corresponding assistant/code result. Use a real small request, for example:

> In DecisionDesk, add the sentence “Use speaker names for clearer ownership” below the live meeting notes field. Keep everything on localhost and do not record audio.

Do not claim this request was used to build the app until you have actually dictated it and the change has been made. A review instruction is also valid evidence of the work actually performed, but describe it as a review rather than code generation.

## Product-input evidence

1. Choose the clearly fictional rehearsal workspace.
2. Open New meeting → Paste transcript (or Live notes).
3. Start screen recording. Focus the meeting field and activate Wispr Flow using the shortcut shown by your installation.
4. Speak the fictional follow-up from Demo-script.md. Capture Flow entering the text and your review of it.
5. Run local AI analysis and show the source evidence, corrected date and conflict review.
6. Capture your explicit approval, followed by the resulting local task. A GitHub preview is not a posted issue.
7. Play the recording back. Confirm video, readable text and intended audio. Keep the original file locally.

Use only fictional/non-sensitive content. Do not record credentials, private notifications, other participants or live calls without appropriate permission. Existing microphone/camera permission prompts remain your choice; no new permissions were accepted by the assistant.

## Before submitting

- Check the current official form and deadline. Previously recorded: project 31 October 2026 11:59 PM IST; LinkedIn links/screenshots 1 November 2026 11:59 PM IST. These need a final confirmation.
- Verify the actual recording duration and any required format. Previous guidance: three-minute demo.
- Replace every missing video/evidence/team field in Submission-draft.md.
- Make the video accessible to judges using a sharing setting you approve. Test playback with the access judges will have.
- Confirm source-code access; a private GitHub link alone does not establish judge access. Do not expose credentials, transcripts or local databases.
- Review the contribution disclosure and distinguish pre-kickoff generic setup from the hackathon implementation.
- If a live tracker test is required, review the exact fictional issue preview and confirm posting yourself. Record the real resulting URL only after success.
- Publish only truthful LinkedIn content, tag the actual Product Space company page, and retain the real post URLs and engagement screenshots. No invented likes/comments or backdated claims.
- Submit the project and LinkedIn materials to their respective portals. Save each confirmation separately.

## What this preparation does not complete

The assistant has not recorded genuine Wispr use, uploaded a video, changed sharing permissions, published LinkedIn posts, invited judges, created a tracker issue or submitted an entry. Those actions need the user's evidence, review or explicit authorization.
