- Open Settings → Connections. Select Local AI · Ollama and Qwen3 1.7B. Complete one warm-up analysis before recording; confirm the app shows an `ollama:` engine on review.
- Choose **Demo rehearsal — fictional** in the workspace picker. It contains only a seeded, approved planning fixture. Approval of that fixture was performed by the rehearsal script, not by a live user. Its launch date is 6 November 2026. Do not reset or delete another workspace to rehearse.

## Follow-up to dictate genuinely

Say speaker labels and punctuation naturally, then inspect Flow’s resulting text before analysis:

Maya: Decision: launch date is 2026-11-08, actually 2026-11-09.
Arjun: I will update the demo by 2026-11-04.

If Flow produces a different spelling or date, review it visibly. Do not pretend a manually pasted transcript was dictated. If AI returns an incorrect owner/date or topic, correct/retry transparently; a valid quote does not guarantee correct interpretation.
